lueur

A sleep diary, simple and private.

Note your nights, nothing more

A few seconds in the morning: when you went to bed, when you got up, the awakenings, how you feel. Each night becomes a band of light, and the weeks can be read at a glance.

  • Everything stays on your phone

    No account, no server, no usage statistics. On Android, the app doesn’t even have permission to access the Internet.

  • No score, no streak

    No mark out of 100, no flame to keep alive. Calm landmarks, shown as averages and over a number of nights.

  • A PDF for your doctor

    The diary exports to a document a health professional can read, over the period you choose.

  • Open source

    The source code is public, under the MIT licence. Anyone can check what the app does.

Not a medical device

Lueur does not diagnose anything and does not replace advice from a health professional. If difficult nights have been going on for several weeks, talking to a doctor can really help. The PDF diary export is made for that.
