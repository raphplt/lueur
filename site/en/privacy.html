Privacy policy

Effective 25 September 2026

Lueur collects no data. What you record stays on your phone.

This page describes exactly what the app does, and what it doesn’t. It applies to the Lueur app for iOS and Android.

Where your data lives

Your nights, markers, notes and settings are stored in a local SQLite database on your device. They are not sent anywhere. The developer has no access to them and receives no information about you, not even the fact that you use the app.

Your phone’s own backups (iCloud, Google) may include the app’s data, depending on your system settings. Those backups are then handled by Apple or Google, not by Lueur.

What Lueur doesn’t have

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • No server.
  • No usage statistics or analytics.
  • No automatic crash reporting.
  • No advertising.
  • No tracking, no advertising identifier, no third-party SDK that talks to the outside world.

On Android, the release build doesn’t even request permission to access the Internet. The app cannot send anything, even by mistake.

Notifications

Reminders (morning, evening) are scheduled on the phone itself. They are local notifications: no push server is involved. You can turn them off in the app or in your system settings.

Export and import

A file (JSON backup, CSV table, PDF diary) is created only when you ask for it. It is passed to the system share sheet, towards the destination you choose: a messaging app, a folder, a printer. From then on, it follows the rules of that destination.

Restoring a backup reads the file you select, on your device. Nothing is downloaded.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings > Your data > Erase everything permanently removes nights, markers, notes and settings from this phone. Uninstalling the app has the same effect.

Since the developer holds no data, there is nothing else to delete on their side.

Children

Lueur is not specifically aimed at children. It collects no data, including from any children who might use it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its effective date. The full history remains available in the source code repository. See the repository.

Should a future version ever handle data differently, this would be stated clearly in the app before any change.
